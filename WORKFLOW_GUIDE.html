A web-based inventory system where staff manage stock by scanning barcodes — no manual data entry for day-to-day work. Every scan instantly updates stock, records who did it and when, and feeds live reports. It works exactly like the checkout and receiving systems at large retail chains.

2 · How it works (the simple idea)

Every product gets its barcode linked once to a catalogue item — the same one-time setup every retail chain does. After that, the barcode acts as an instant lookup key: scan it, and the system already knows the product, its category, stock, and batches. Scanning reads the number and looks it up — that is the entire engine behind D-Mart and Walmart too.

Honest note for review: No system "reads product details out of a barcode" — a barcode is only an ID number. Large stores build a complete catalogue once, then scan against it forever. We have done the same: the catalogue is loaded; the only remaining step is linking each product's barcode to it (one time).

3 · The workflow

One-time setup — link the barcodes

1
Open the Scan screen → SETUP mode
A dedicated mode just for linking — no stock changes.
2
Scan a product → pick the matching item (1 click)
The system shows the catalogue; you confirm which product the barcode belongs to.
3
Barcode bonded — forever
Walk the shelves once. Each product is now permanently recognised.

Faster option: scan all barcodes into one spreadsheet and the system auto-links every product it can identify, leaving only the rest to confirm.

Daily operations — just scan

🔗 SETUP
Link barcodes (one-time, per product)
↙ INWARD
Scan to ADD stock when goods arrive
↗ OUTWARD
Scan to REMOVE stock (oldest batch first)
↙
Goods arrive → INWARD mode → scan
Stock goes up. A batch is created with date (and expiry if printed on a carton). Logged under the staff member.
↗
Goods leave → OUTWARD mode → scan
Stock goes down using FIFO (oldest stock first), so nothing expires sitting on the shelf.
📊
Reports update live
Current stock, low-stock alerts, expiry alerts, margins and a full audit trail — all automatic.
