📷 The Barcode System

A super-simple guide. If you can use a phone, you can use this.

First, what is a barcode?

A barcode is just those black lines printed on every product. When you scan it, the computer reads a number — that's all it is. A number. Nothing else. It does not contain the product's name or price.

📱 Think of it exactly like your phone contacts.
A phone number by itself is just digits. But you save it once with a name — "Ramesh" — and after that, every time Ramesh calls, your phone shows "Ramesh", not the number.

Our system is the same: we teach it once — "this barcode = this product" — and after that, every scan shows the product automatically.

There are only 3 buttons

Open the app, go to the Scan page. You will see three big buttons at the top:

🔗
SETUP
Teach the computer (do this once)
⬇️
INWARD
Stock coming IN
⬆️
OUTWARD
Stock going OUT

That's it. Pick a button, then scan. The colour tells you what you're doing: purple = teach, green = in, orange = out.

🔗 Button 1 — SETUP (teach the computer)

You do this ONCE for each product. Like saving a contact in your phone.

1
Click the purple SETUP button.
2
Scan a product's barcode.
The computer says: "I don't know this one yet."
3
A list of your products appears. Tap the right one.
Type a few letters of the name to find it fast. Tap it.
4
Done! That barcode is now saved forever. ✅
Scan the next product. Keep going down your shelves.

You never have to do this again for that product.

⬇️ Button 2 — INWARD (stock coming in)

Use this when new goods arrive in the shop.

1
Click the green INWARD button.
2
Type how many (the Quantity box). Example: 10.
3
Scan the product.
Stock goes UP by 10. The screen shows it. Done.
What you see on screen
📷 scan here…
✅ +10   "now 35 in stock"

⬆️ Button 3 — OUTWARD (stock going out)

Use this when goods leave the shop (sold, used, dispatched).

1
Click the orange OUTWARD button.
2
Type how many. Example: 4.
3
Scan the product.
Stock goes DOWN by 4. The computer automatically takes the oldest stock first, so nothing gets too old.
What you see on screen
📷 scan here…
⬆️ −4   "now 31 in stock"

A normal day in the shop

🌅
Morning: a delivery arrives.
Press INWARD, type the count, scan each item. Stock goes up.
🛒
During the day: goods go out.
Press OUTWARD, type the count, scan. Stock goes down.
📊
Anytime: the boss checks reports.
Stock levels, low-stock warnings, expiry alerts — all update by themselves.

What if it says "I don't know this barcode"?

No problem — it just means that product was never taught yet. A small box appears: pick the right product from the list, tap it, and it's saved. Next time, it will be recognised instantly. It's the same as saving a new contact in your phone.

The only rules to remember

  1. First, teach each product once using the purple SETUP button.
  2. Goods coming in? → Green INWARD button.
  3. Goods going out? → Orange OUTWARD button.
  4. Always type the quantity first, then scan.
  5. Click inside the scan box so the scanner knows where to type.

That's the whole system. Teach once → then just scan in and scan out. 🎉
